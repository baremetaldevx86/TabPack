# TabPack pack-training throughput

One pack of K MLPs (`ModelPack` + packed optimizer; one step = every member
takes one step on its own batch) vs the same K MLPs trained one at a time
(K separate `K=1` packs). Generated by `scripts/benchmark_pack.py`.

* **ms/step**: median wall-clock time for all K members to take one step
  (sequential: sum of the K members' step times).
* **samples/s**: K x batch_size rows per step / step time.
* **speedup**: sequential ms/step / pack ms/step (medians).
* **speedup (min)**: the same ratio of the fastest blocks, which is less
  sensitive to CPU contention from other processes (the steps are
  launch-bound, so a busy CPU slows them down).
* **peak MiB**: `torch.cuda.max_memory_allocated` over the configuration
  (sequential: models live one at a time).
* At K = 1 both sides run identical work, so the distance of the K = 1
  speedup from 1.00x shows the measurement noise of the run.

## Environment

* device: `cuda` (NVIDIA GeForce RTX 5050 Laptop GPU, 8,151 MiB)
* torch 2.11.0+cu128, CUDA 12.8, cuDNN 91900, Python 3.12.3
* platform: Linux-6.18.33.2-microsoft-standard-WSL2-x86_64-with-glibc2.43; TF32 matmul: False
* git commit: `3d693d9`; run at 2026-09-24T17:08:49+00:00
* host load: 1-min load average 4.85 at start, 3.67 at the end (12 logical CPUs)

## Configuration

* data: 6400 random rows shaped like Churn: 7 numerical + categorical cardinalities [3, 2, 2, 2] -> d_in = 16; binary target, BCE loss
* model: d_block = 384, n_blocks = 3, dropout = 0.1, ReLU; 302,593 parameters per member
* batch: 256 rows per member per step; warmup 5 steps, then 11 repeats x 10 steps (median)
* sequential baseline: measured (K separate K=1 packs, one at a time) for K <= 8; for larger K, K x the mean time of the 8 measured members

## Headline (largest K)

* AdamWPack, float32: K = 32 -> 32.52x faster than sequential (10.54 vs 342.69 ms per step of all members; 30.61x from the fastest blocks)
* AdamWPack, bf16 autocast: K = 32 -> 25.80x faster than sequential (8.09 vs 208.63 ms per step of all members; 27.86x from the fastest blocks)
* MuonAdamWPack, float32: K = 32 -> 12.32x faster than sequential (22.34 vs 275.08 ms per step of all members; 11.96x from the fastest blocks)
* MuonAdamWPack, bf16 autocast: K = 32 -> 15.36x faster than sequential (22.12 vs 339.74 ms per step of all members; 15.57x from the fastest blocks)
